# DESIGN LAWS — the anti-generic gate

Ten checks that catch a sheet that looks like every other AI graphic on the feed. Any single
fail is a fail.

---

### 1. Exactly one spotlight
**Fail:** no lit block, or two.
**Why:** the spotlight is her whole signature: a sheet that picks a winner. Zero is a generic
grid; two is two answers, which is no answer.
**Fix:** decide what matters most and light only that. If nothing matters most, the content is
a list, not a sheet. Re-cut it.

### 2. No icon that restates the title
**Fail:** a pen icon on "Hooks in your own voice".
**Why:** the fastest tell of a generated design.
**Fix:** delete it. Her numeral cards need no icons at all.

### 3. No neon, glass, or stray glow
**Fail:** glowing outlines on quiet cards, frosted panels, a glow behind the headline.
**Why:** on a dark stage these are the cheapest moves available, and they dilute the one glow
that should exist: the spotlight's.
**Fix:** quiet cards are flat Char with a 1px Line. Only the spotlight glows.

### 4. Fire is the only colour
**Fail:** a blue link, a green tick, a purple tag.
**Why:** one colour on black is what makes her sheet readable from across the feed.
**Fix:** carry meaning with position, weight and the dimmed ghost style, not with a hue.

### 5. Type floor and scale
**Fail:** text under 12px, or more than five sizes.
**Fix:** cut words. Never shrink type to fit.

### 6. No filler cards
**Fail:** a sixth card that exists because the grid has six slots.
**Fix:** use four cards, or switch to Hero + Stack.

### 7. No emoji on the canvas
**Fix:** delete. If it carried meaning, that meaning needs words.

### 8. No anonymous headline
**Fail:** "Powerful AI Tools Every Creator Should Know."
**Why:** it fits anyone's sheet.
**Fix:** bold subject, regular promise, one fire word for the outcome. If you can swap her name
for a competitor's and it still fits, it is not her headline yet.

### 9. No light canvas
**Fail:** a white or pastel ground.
**Why:** the dark stage is half her identity. A light Javeriya sheet is someone else's sheet.
**Fix:** Night ground, always.

### 10. Nothing that reads as a neighbouring account's system
**Fail:** a structural line threading the cards together; pastel card families with tonal
borders; the sheet drawn as a physical object (a phone, a folder, a terminal).
**Why:** each of those is the signature of another account in the same or an adjacent niche.
Borrowing one makes her look derivative of exactly the accounts she competes with.
**Fix:** her cards stand on a stage and one of them is lit. That is the whole idea.

---

## Running the gate

```
1  one spotlight            PASS / FAIL
2  redundant icons          PASS / FAIL
3  neon / glass / glow      PASS / FAIL
4  fire is the only colour  PASS / FAIL
5  type floor and scale     PASS / FAIL
6  filler cards             PASS / FAIL
7  emoji                    PASS / FAIL
8  anonymous headline       PASS / FAIL
9  light canvas             PASS / FAIL
10 someone else's system    PASS / FAIL
```

## The test behind all ten

**Cover the footer. Could this be anyone's sheet?** If yes, it has failed, whatever the checks
said. The dark stage, the one lit block and the fire word should answer that question on
their own.
