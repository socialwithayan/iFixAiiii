# THE WIN MATRIX

Twelve aspects, scored 1–5 on the rendered PNG at 100% and again at phone size.

**The bar: nothing below 4. Aspects 2, 11 and 12 must be 5.**

Score what is on the screen, not what you meant.

---

### 1. Hierarchy
- **5**: headline, then the spotlight, then the cards, then the closer. In that order, without effort.
- **4**: right order, but one quiet card pulls too hard.
- **3**: you have to work out where to look.
- **1–2**: everything shouts.

*Squint test: blur your eyes. You should see the headline and one lit block, and nothing else.*

### 2. Spotlight earned — must be 5
- **5**: exactly one lit block. It is the most important thing on the sheet, `WHY LIT` is
  obvious from the content, and it still wins in the grey render.
- **4 or below**: lit for balance, wins only on colour, or sitting top-left where it reads as
  "first" rather than "chosen". **Fail.**

### 3. Stage
- **5**: Night ground, quiet charcoal cards, the ember glow low and behind. Clean.
- **4**: the glow is slightly visible as a shape.
- **3 or below**: the glow muddies text, or the stage reads grey rather than black.

### 4. Contrast
- **5**: every text clears its floor. Lit cards use **Fire-lit** (`#D60802 → #E62602`), so white
  body copy is 4.52:1 or better end to end. The full Fire gradient only carries the fire word,
  the pill and the send circle.
- **3 or below**: a lit card on the full Fire gradient with body copy over the orange end
  (down to 3.34:1). **Fail.**

### 5. Type split
- **5**: bold subject, regular promise, one Fraunces fire word, and the fire word is the
  outcome ("Noticed", "Chosen"), not the topic noun ("AI", "LinkedIn").
- **4**: the split is there, but the fire word is a weaker choice than it could be.
- **3 or below**: no split, or two fire words.

### 6. Alignment
- **5**: card edges, numerals and baselines exactly on the grid.
- **4**: one element a few px out.
- **3 or below**: visible raggedness.

### 7. Fire budget
- **5**: gradient in four places at most (fire word, spotlight, tag pill, send circle). Solid
  Ember once as a figure and once as a label, at most.
- **4**: one extra Ember use.
- **3 or below**: fire on body text, a fifth gradient, or a second accent colour. **Fail.**

### 8. Icon discipline
- **5**: every icon adds information, or there are none. None scores 5.
- **3 or below**: an icon per card restating the title.

### 9. Edge safety
- **5**: nothing clipped, margins even.
- **3 or below**: something crosses the edge. **Fail.**

### 10. Motion
Only when a GIF or MP4 ships.
- **5**: frame one is already a full sheet (headline, pill, footer). Quiet cards arrive, then
  the spotlight visibly ignites. Two movers, chrome still.
- **4**: the ignition is a little fast to read at feed size.
- **3 or below**: frame one is blank, or the spotlight is lit from the start, so the ignition
  never happens.

### 11. Footer — must be 5
- **5**: her photo, "Follow Javeriya A. For More", "DM for collaborations", the fire send circle,
  pinned at 1306, full width.
- **anything else**: **Fail.**

### 12. Fact integrity — must be 5
- **5**: every tool name, feature, price, model and stat checked this run, with source and date.
- **anything else**: **Fail.**

## The two ways this gate gets cheated

1. **Scoring from memory.** Open the PNG.
2. **Rounding up.** A 3 becomes a 4 because the rework is annoying. That is exactly the sheet
   the feed ignores.
