# LAUNCH POSTS — the copy that ships with the sheet

LinkedIn is her home platform; X is secondary. Every sheet ships with both, LinkedIn written
first. **The post carries the idea; the sheet carries the detail.**

Voice rules: `voice.md`.

---

## LinkedIn (primary)

**Shape**

```
[hook: one line, a claim or a contrast. Most people X. The top 10% Y.]

[2-3 short lines: the problem in their week]

[the turn: the one thing that changes it, which is the sheet's spotlight]

[3-5 single-line items: the headline version of the sheet's cards]

[one closing line: what to do now]

[the sheet: MP4 if the ignition adds something, otherwise the PNG]
```

**Worked example**

```
Most people use AI to post more. The top 10% use it to get noticed.

More posts did not grow anyone's profile.
Posts that the right people stop for did.

Start with the one thing every visitor reads first: your headline.

Hooks in your own voice
A comment radar on ten buyer accounts
One idea, cut into a carousel
Last month's best post, rewritten for this month

Pick one and run it daily for 30 days. The full breakdown is in the sheet.
```

**Rules**

- The post names the spotlight. The sheet lit it for a reason; say the reason.
- One clause per line, single-line items, no paragraph bullets.
- One ask at the end, or none.
- Sponsored? Say so plainly in the post. "Partnered with [brand] on this one" is enough.

## X (secondary)

```
[the claim, concrete]
[the turn: the spotlight, in one line]

[the sheet: GIF]
```

```
Most people use AI to post more.
The top 10% use it to rewrite one thing first: their headline.
```

Under 280 characters. The GIF goes on X; the ignition earns the stop.

---

## Both posts

- **Never describe the design.** Not the colours, not the layout.
- **Numbers in the post match the sheet exactly.**
- **One claim per post.** Two ideas means two sheets.
- **No "follow me" CTA.** The footer already says it.

## Hook shapes that fit her

```
Most people [X]. The top 10% [Y].
I tested [N] AI tools for LinkedIn this month. [One] was worth keeping.
If you only fix one thing on your profile, fix this.
Same tools everyone has. Used the way almost nobody uses them.
You do not need more AI tools. You need one workflow, done daily.
```

Rotate them. The same opener three weeks running trains people to scroll past.
