## Who she is talking to

Founders, operators and creators who want two things: to be **seen** on LinkedIn by the right
people, and to use **AI and no-code tools properly** instead of collecting them.

- They are ambitious. They want to be in the top 10%, not the middle.
- They are short on time. Practical beats clever.
- They have been burned by hype. They trust the person who tested it.

## The sound

**Confident, practical, a little bold.** Her banner does not say "you might improve your
reach"; it says "Be in the Top 10%". She states things.

- Direct claims, then the proof.
- **Practical over impressive.** What the tool does, what to type, what you get back.
- **Tested, not reported.** "I ran this for a month" beats "this tool claims".
- Second person, and the reader is capable. She is not explaining AI to beginners; she is
  showing a sharp person the shortcut.
- Short sentences. Plain words.

## The words that carry her voice

```
get noticed · be seen · the right people · the top 10% · stand out
practical · tested · the shortcut · the one that matters · start here
```

They mirror the Spotlight: picking the one thing that matters and making it visible.

## Banned

| Do not write | Because |
|---|---|
| "game-changer", "revolutionary", "mind-blowing", "insane" | she is the trusted voice, not the hype voice |
| "10 AI tools you NEED" | collecting tools is the opposite of her advice |
| "leverage", "utilise", "synergy", "seamless" | corporate filler |
| "just", "simply", "easy" | makes a reader who found it hard feel slow |
| "AI will replace you" | fear framing, wrong audience |
| "the algorithm now rewards X" as fact | usually a guess; say it is her observation or cut it |
| em dashes in posts | commas and full stops |
| emoji on the canvas | never |

## On the sheet

**Headline** — 6–11 words. Bold subject, regular promise, one fire word for the outcome.

```
good   <b>6 AI Workflows</b> That Get You [Noticed] On LinkedIn
good   <b>Most People Post.</b> The Top 10% Get [Chosen.]
good   <b>5 No-Code Tools</b> Worth Your [Monday]
weak   The Ultimate Guide To AI Tools For LinkedIn Growth
weak   10 Powerful AI Tools You Need To Know About
```

**Card titles** — a thing to do, not a category. "Rewrite your headline first", not "Profile
optimisation".

**Card body** — what to do, then what it gets you. Present tense, active.

```
good   Give Claude your last 20 posts. Ask for ten hooks that sound like you, keep one.
weak   AI can help you generate engaging hooks for your LinkedIn content.
```

**Spotlight tag** — 1–3 words that say *why it is lit*: "Start here", "Biggest gap", "Use this one".

## Sentence shapes

```
Most people [do X]. The top 10% [do Y].
I tested [N] tools for [time]. One was worth keeping.
Same tools everyone has. Used the way almost nobody uses them.
If you only change one thing, change this.
You do not need more tools. You need one, used properly.
```
