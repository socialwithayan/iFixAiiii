Your posts send people here. These 8 checks decide if they stay.

Banner works on mobile
1584 × 396. Keep the message in the centre. Phones crop the sides and your photo covers the bottom-left.
2
Photo is close and clear
It shows as a circle. Centre your face and upload bigger than 400 × 400 so it stays sharp.

Fix this firstHeadline says who you help
It follows you into search, comments and invites. Only the first 60–70 of 220 characters show. Lead with it.
4
About opens with them
About 300 of 2,600 characters show before "see more". Spend them on their problem, not your history.
5
Featured points to one offer
Pin the post, link or document you want visitors to act on. Not an old announcement.
6
Your URL is your name
linkedin.com/in/yourname, not a string of numbers. It goes on every deck and email signature.
7
Experience reads as results
Each role opens with what changed because of you, not a list of duties.
8
One clear way to reach you
A working link in Featured or contact info. Someone ready to buy should not have to search.
