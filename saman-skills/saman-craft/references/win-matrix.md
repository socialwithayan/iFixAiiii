# THE WIN MATRIX

Twelve aspects, scored 1–5 on the rendered PNG at 100% and again at phone size.

**The bar: nothing below 4. Aspects 2, 11 and 12 must be 5** — spine integrity, the footer and
the facts are pass/fail wearing a score, and a 4 on any of them is a fail.

Score what is on the screen, not what you meant.

---

### 1. Hierarchy
Where does the eye go, in what order?

- **5** — headline, then the shape of the sheet, then a card, then the detail. In that order, without effort.
- **4** — right order, but one element fights the headline.
- **3** — you have to work out where to start.
- **1–2** — everything shouts, or nothing does.

*Squint test: blur your eyes. The headline and the spine should still be the two things you see.*

### 2. Spine integrity — must be 5
- **5** — one line, unbroken; every block attached or stubbed; obvious start and end.
- **4 or below** — a break, a floating card, or an ambiguous direction. **Fail.**

*Trace it with a finger. If you lift, it fails.*

### 3. Density
- **5** — roughly 60% ground, reads at phone size, nothing crowded.
- **4** — a little tight in one band.
- **3** — packed; you notice the effort of reading it.
- **1–2** — wall of content. Cut cards, do not shrink type.

### 4. Contrast
- **5** — every text block clears its floor, Blue carries no meaning, nothing relies on colour alone.
- **4** — one caption is doing too much work in `--ink-45`.
- **3 or below** — text on Blue, or meaning carried by colour. **Fail.**

### 5. Type rhythm
- **5** — four sizes or fewer, consistent line heights, no orphan word, no cramped wrap.
- **4** — one awkward wrap.
- **3** — sizes drifting off the scale.

### 6. Alignment
- **5** — everything on the grid. Card edges, node centres, text baselines.
- **4** — one element a few px out.
- **3 or below** — visible raggedness.

*Drop a straight edge down the card column and down the spine. Both should be exact.*

### 7. Colour ratio and roles
- **5** — 60 Sky / 30 fills / 10 Ink. Blue only on the spine. Bright in at most three places
  (rings, chip text, closing figure). Every fill pale enough that the set reads as one family.
- **4** — Ink creeping past 10%, or a fourth use of Bright.
- **3 or below** — Blue filling an area, Bright filling a card, a pastel carrying text, or a
  hex outside the named set. **Fail.**

*Squint at it: you should see five colours, not ten. If the cards read as five separate
decisions rather than one family, a fill is too saturated.*

### 8. Icon discipline
- **5** — every icon adds information, or there are none at all.
- **4** — one decorative icon.
- **3 or below** — an icon per card restating the title. That is the single most generic move available.

*No icons at all scores 5. Icons are not required; earning their place is.*

### 9. Edge safety
- **5** — nothing clipped, margins even, footer clean.
- **4** — one element tight to the margin.
- **3 or below** — something crosses the edge. **Fail.**

### 10. Motion legibility
Only when a GIF or MP4 is shipping.

- **5** — the spine draw reads at feed size, the sequence is followable, the rest frame is the sheet.
- **4** — slightly fast, still readable.
- **3 or below** — a blur, or the first frame is nearly empty so the feed shows nothing.

*The first frame is what a paused feed shows. It must not be blank.*

### 11. Footer — must be 5
- **5** — "Follow Saman Ahmed For More" + @ScaleWthAI, avatar present, pinned at 1306, full width, Ink.
- **anything else** — **Fail.** The footer is locked. It is what makes forty sheets one body of work.

### 12. Fact integrity — must be 5
- **5** — every number, name, price and claim checked this run, with a source and a date.
- **anything else** — **Fail.** Not "probably right". Checked.

*Her entire position is that her systems actually work. One wrong number costs more than the sheet earns.*

## Scoring it

```
1  Hierarchy          _/5
2  Spine integrity    _/5   must be 5
3  Density            _/5
4  Contrast           _/5
5  Type rhythm        _/5
6  Alignment          _/5
7  Colour ratio       _/5
8  Icon discipline    _/5
9  Edge safety        _/5
10 Motion legibility  _/5   (n/a if static only)
11 Footer             _/5   must be 5
12 Fact integrity     _/5   must be 5
                      ----
VERDICT   ship / rework: <the specific thing>
```

A verdict of "rework" names one specific change. "Make it better" is not a verdict.

## The two ways this gate gets cheated

1. **Scoring from memory.** You looked at the sheet ten minutes ago and you score from
   recollection. Open the PNG.
2. **Rounding up.** A 3 becomes a 4 because the rework is annoying. That is exactly the sheet
   the feed ignores, and the annoyance was ten minutes.
