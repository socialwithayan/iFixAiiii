# LAUNCH POSTS — the copy that ships with the sheet

Every finished sheet ships with two posts: one for X, one for LinkedIn. Same idea, different
shape — a post copy-pasted across both reads like neither.

**The rule that matters:** the post carries the idea, the sheet carries the detail. A post that
narrates the image ("Here are 5 systems 👇") wastes the only thing people read.

Voice rules: `voice.md`. Read them first.

---

## X

Short. The first line has to survive a scroll with the image collapsed.

**Shape**

```
[line 1 — the claim, concrete, no wind-up]
[line 2 — the turn: what most people do instead, or what this replaces]

[optional: one line of proof or the honest caveat]

[the sheet: PNG or GIF]
```

**Worked example**

```
Five workflows take ten hours a week off a founder's calendar.

Not five new tools. The five you already pay for, set up once so they run
without you.

Full breakdown in the sheet.
```

**Rules**

- No "thread 🧵", no "🔥", no "let me break it down".
- No numbered list in the post — that is what the sheet is for.
- Under 280 characters where you can. If it needs more, it needs an article, not a longer post.
- The GIF goes on X. Motion earns the stop.

## LinkedIn

More room, more context, same restraint. Founders read LinkedIn at their desk.

**Shape**

```
[hook — one line, the outcome or the cost of not having it]

[2-3 short lines — the specific problem in their week]

[the turn — what changes when it runs by itself]

[3-5 single-line items, the headline version of the sheet's cards]

[closing line — what to do with this]

[the sheet: PNG, or the MP4 if the motion adds something]
```

**Worked example**

```
Most founders lose a full working day a week to admin they never chose.

It is not the big things. It is the inbox at 7am, the meeting notes nobody
writes up, the Friday report you rebuild from scratch every week.

None of that needs you. It needs setting up once.

Inbox triage and first drafts — 2.5 hrs
Meeting notes into owned actions — 1.5 hrs
One idea cut for every channel — 2 hrs
The weekly report, assembled — 2 hrs
First-pass candidate screening — 2 hrs

Ten hours. Built once, running every week, without hiring anyone.

The full setup for each one is in the sheet.
```

**Rules**

- No "👇" and no "Comment 'SYSTEM' and I'll send it" unless she actually wants the DMs.
- Single-line items, one clause each. No paragraph bullets.
- The MP4 on LinkedIn, not the GIF — it autoplays and holds quality.
- One ask at the end, or none. Never two.

---

## Both posts

- **Never mention the design.** Not the layout, not the colours, not that it took a while.
  The sheet speaks for itself and talking about it makes it smaller.
- **The numbers in the post match the sheet exactly.** A mismatch is the fastest way to look
  careless, and both are screenshotted together.
- **One claim per post.** If there are two ideas, there are two posts and two sheets.
- **No CTA to follow.** The footer already says it. Saying it twice is desperate.

## Hook shapes that fit her

```
[N] workflows take [X hours] a week off a founder's calendar.
Most founders try to save time by adding tools. This removes steps.
The [30-minute] setup that kills your Friday admin.
You already do this every week. Here is how it runs without you.
The part of your week you hate is the part that automates cleanest.
```

Rotate them. The same opener three weeks running trains people to scroll past it.
